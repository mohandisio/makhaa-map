About this data

Yemeni coffee houses — serving qahwa, qishr, adeni chai and honeycomb bread — have been opening across the US at a remarkable pace in the last few years. This project tracks that wave as a census: every location of every chain we know about, in one place.

The data is captured directly from each brand's own public store locator, so the brands themselves are the source of record. Addresses are normalized, deduplicated, and geocoded against the US Census and OpenStreetMap Nominatim geocoders; the handful of stores without a working locator are curated by hand. Only what this page displays is published — the underlying dataset stays private.

Next up: weekly snapshots build a history, turning this page from one frame into a time-lapse — store counts over time, openings per month, and how each brand's footprint spreads.

Data comes from each brand's public store locator; positions from the locators themselves or the US Census / Nominatim geocoders.
